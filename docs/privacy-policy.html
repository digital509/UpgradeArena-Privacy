Upgrade Arena Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Winged Spur Games ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use Upgrade Arena (the "Game") on Meta Horizon devices.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the Game may process:

  • Meta account information: Meta User ID and Horizon username. We use the username for player identification, such as player nameplates and multiplayer menus. We do not currently display the Meta profile photo.
  • Social and multiplayer information: friends-list availability, presence, invitations, destination and deep-link information needed to find, invite, and join eligible friends.
  • Gameplay and session information: authentication identifiers, lobby and relay identifiers, match membership, player color, scores, kills, game state, and networked player or robot movement.
  • Voice information: microphone audio when voice chat is enabled. Voice is processed through Unity Vivox to provide real-time communication. Voice audio is intended to be ephemeral and is not recorded or stored by Winged Spur Games.
  • Device and technical information: information required by Meta, Unity, and Vivox to operate their services, which may include device identifiers, truncated IP address, connection diagnostics, and performance information.
  • Tracking input: headset and controller position or rotation used to provide VR movement, aiming, and multiplayer representation. We use this information for real-time gameplay and do not use it for advertising.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • authenticate players and maintain consistent multiplayer identity;
  • create, discover, join, and operate public or private multiplayer sessions;
  • display usernames and gameplay information to other players;
  • support friends, invitations, presence, destinations, and deep-link joining;
  • provide voice chat and player mute controls;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Game; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not use Meta Horizon User Data or Device User Data for targeted advertising.

3. Service Providers and Data Sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the Game with:

  • Meta Platforms: platform authentication, User ID, profile, friends, invitations, presence, destinations, deep linking, and Horizon device services.
  • Unity Technologies / Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Lobby, Relay, multiplayer infrastructure, and related technical operations.
  • Unity Vivox: real-time voice communications and associated service operations.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. They may use cloud subprocessors and may process information in countries including Australia, Belgium, Brazil, Germany, Israel, Singapore, Taiwan, and the United States, depending on the service and routing used.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users, or to investigate fraud, security incidents, or abuse.

4. Data Retention

We retain developer-controlled personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, to maintain the Game, or to satisfy applicable legal requirements. Multiplayer lobby, relay, presence, and voice information may be transient or retained according to the applicable service provider's retention practices. Vivox states that voice data is ephemeral by default and that certain identifiers and connection information may be retained for limited periods.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may mute voice chat or deny microphone access through the Game or Meta device controls.
  • You may control friends, presence, invitations, and platform privacy through your Meta account settings.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information controlled by Winged Spur Games by contacting us at privacy@wingedspurgames.com.

To help protect players, we may ask for enough information to verify the account connected to a request. We will delete or de-identify information we control when required, and will direct or forward applicable requests to Unity or Vivox when their assistance is necessary. Deleting your Meta account or Unity-related data may require a separate request to the applicable provider.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including authenticated access and the security controls supplied by Meta and Unity. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Upgrade Arena is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. International Processing

The Game's service providers operate internationally. Information may be processed outside your country of residence where data-protection laws may differ. Our providers are responsible for applying appropriate safeguards to their processing as required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game or its services change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate notice when required.
